Different Uses of "Bring"

Meaning Guide

• bring = to take something or someone to the place where the speaker is
• bring along = to take someone or something with you (informal)
• bring over = to take something to another person’s home or place
• bring up = to raise a child OR to start talking about a topic

Grammar Tip:
• bring (base form)
• brings (he / she / it)
• brought (past form)
