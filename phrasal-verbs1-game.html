Phrasal Verb Bring Up and Throw Up

Bring up means to care for a child until they are an adult; or, in professional contexts, to mention or introduce a topic in conversation.

Throw up (simple past: threw up) means to vomit; informally, it can also mean to produce something quickly or reveal new information.
